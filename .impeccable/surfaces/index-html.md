Scope: the single study screen (index.html). Visitor mode: Operate.
Task: start/pause focus blocks, pick and tick subject tasks, glance at the week's streak. Used nightly in exam season, desk and phone.
Constraints: pinned palette, fonts and neo-brutalist style (see PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments); AA contrast; full keyboard flow.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A study desk built like a stack of chunky printed blocks: the timer is one huge teal slab you can hit with a spacebar. Refuses the soft rounded wellness-app timer with a thin ring.

OWN-WORLD: Misty #EEF4F2 ground, navy ink #14213D 3px rules and 6px hard shadows on every block, teal slab for the timer, mint for "on" and "done", apricot only on the streak badge and finish bursts. Archivo Black digits, Space Grotesk everything else. Buttons press down into their shadow.

STORY: Student opens the page, sees the time, hits Space, works. Ticks a task, it gets slammed through with a mint strike. Finishing a block lights today's square in the streak grid.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: teal timer slab fills ~60% width left, digits ~13rem, mode switch above, Start/Reset/Skip below, current task line; right column the checklist, stretched to end level with the left column. Streak grid below the slab in the left column (amended after finish review: full width left ~500px of dead ground beside the grid and under the slab, because the checklist is the tallest block). Phone: timer first, full width.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — "neo-brutalist — thick 3px borders and hard 6px offset shadows in a deep navy ink (#14213D), flat color blocks on a cool misty base (#EEF4F2)"; "a giant Pomodoro timer as the hero, a subject checklist that strikes through with a punchy animation, and a weekly streak grid". Signature interaction: the strike-through slam. Motion grammar: short overshoot on press/complete only; nothing moves while the timer runs.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
